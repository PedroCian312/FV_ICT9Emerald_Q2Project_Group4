O.B. Montessori Center



O.B. Montessori Center or OBMC is a progressive pre-k to 12 co-educational school.


OBMC's mission is to help their students into self-developing and productive individuals who can lead in the future.


Explore the programs and enjoy our vibrant and peaceful community that makes OBMC a very unique place for a child to grow and develop.
